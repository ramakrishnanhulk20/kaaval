---
title: Read the record
---

The site has one job: show you what the engine did, in the order it did it, with nothing added.
Three screens carry most of it.

## The scoreboard

On the home page, scroll to `#record`.

One row per brain. Equity now and the equity curve behind it, realised profit before unrealised,
drawdown from the peak, profit on the day, open positions, fills, refusals, and the last decision
the brain wrote with the time it wrote it. A brain that is halted says so.

Realised comes before unrealised on every line on purpose. An unrealised number in a thin book is
partly your own footprint, so the number you can spend is the one that leads.

Every figure on the row is computed from ledger entries: the curve is every `mark` entry for that
brain, the counts are `fill` and `reject` entries, the summary is the newest `decision`, and open
positions and the halted flag come from the engine state file.

## The timeline

`/timeline?day=2026-09-11`

One UTC day drawn as time, with a lane per brain. Every tick sits where it happened, and inside a
tick you get, per brain: the decision with its summary, targets, rationale, model calls and how long
it took; the refusals with the rule that refused them; the orders with the Agent Hub dry-run request
and the stop request built with them; the fills with price, quantity, fee, slippage and the hash of
the book they filled against; and the closing mark.

A day picker at the top lists every day the ledger holds. Gaps are drawn as gaps. When the machine
that runs the engine was off, the line does not get smoothed over the hole.

11 September 2026 is the night to look at first. The kill switch was pulled live at 08:42:20Z, two
orders were refused under the rule `safety.kill` while it was on, and it was released at 08:59:09Z.
All three sit on the line where they happened.

## A decision

Click any decision on the timeline, or go to `/decisions/<seq>` where `<seq>` is the ledger sequence
number.

A decision page is one brain, one tick, end to end:

- what it proposed, as targets: symbol, signed target size, what it hedges, its own rationale,
  confidence and horizon
- the world it was looking at, taken from the snapshot entries just before it: the symbols with
  their divergence and spread
- the verdict the rulebook gave each target, including the ones it cut down and the ones it refused
  with the rule named
- the Agent Hub dry-run request built from whatever survived
- the fill, with the book hash it was priced against

## The rest

- `/refusals`: every refusal across brains, with counts per rule and a filter.
- `/rulebook`: the rulebook text read back out of the ledger, with the entry and time it was
  written, plus every later change.
- `/proof`: the three checks and a button that re-runs them. See
  [verify the record](/docs/getting-started/verify-the-record).
- `/api/trades.csv`: the six-field trade log, streamed straight off disk.

The word simulated appears on the scoreboard, the timeline, the trade log and the proof panel,
because it is true on all four.
