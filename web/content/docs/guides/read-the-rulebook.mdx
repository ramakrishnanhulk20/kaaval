**Where:** `/rulebook` on the site, `docs/rulebook.md` in the repo, and `src/risk/rulebook.ts` as
data.

The three are the same rulebook seen three ways. The file in `src/risk/rulebook.ts` is the one the
code enforces. `docs/rulebook.md` is the long form with the reasoning behind each number. The page
on the site is neither: it is the rulebook text read back out of the ledger, so what you see there
is what the brains were actually handed.

## The limits

| Limit | Default | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Per symbol | 10 percent of equity | Order cut down to the cap |
| Gross exposure | 60 percent of equity | Cut down |
| Net exposure | 40 percent of equity | Cut down |
| Open positions | 8 | Refused |
| Order size | 100 to 500 USDT | Below is refused, above is cut down |
| Daily loss | 2 percent of start-of-day equity | Reduce-only for the rest of the UTC day |
| Drawdown from peak | 8 percent | Every size halved |
| Drawdown from peak | 12 percent | Flat everything and halt until a human restarts |
| Stop on every position | 3 percent on rTokens, 2 percent on perpetuals | Placed with the entry |
| Spread | At most 30 basis points | Refused |
| Divergence for a new unhedged long | At most 1 percent from the last regular close | Refused |
| Share of the visible book | At most 25 percent | Refused |
| No entries | 09:25 to 09:40 ET, when Bitget's guidance says prices re-anchor | Refused |
| Pre-open | No new unhedged rToken long in the last 30 minutes when divergence is over 0.5 percent | Refused |
| Cross-asset hedge | Any rToken over 5 percent of equity carries a hedge of at least half its delta when divergence is over 0.5 percent or New York is shut for more than 24 hours | Hedge required |
| Universe | 200,000 USDT minimum 24 hour volume, traded through the last weekend, has a matching perpetual | Anything else is refused, not resized |
| Cadence | 15 minutes normally, 2 minutes for 60 minutes after an event and 30 minutes either side of the open | |

## What the site page adds

- **The exact text in force**, with the ledger entry number and the time it was written. That is
  the text both language-model brains were given, so you are reading the prompt, not a summary of
  it.
- **Every later version.** A config entry that repeats the same text is counted and not listed
  again, so the page shows changes rather than one row per engine restart.
- **The universe and the brains** recorded in the same entry, and the public key the run signed
  with.

## Who enforces what

The rulebook is enforced by `src/risk/limits.ts`, which is pure functions over an account, a world
state and the rulebook object. It has no network access and no model access, and it sees no headline
text. It runs before the brains, on the risk-cutting orders, and again after them, on whatever they
proposed.

Size limits cut an order down and record the cut. Everything else refuses the order and records the
rule. Orders that only cut risk, a reduce-only order or the kill switch flattening the book, skip
the entry gates and the order size band, because refusing to shrink a position is how a risk layer
traps a loss instead of stopping one.

To change a number, see [change a limit](/docs/guides/change-a-limit).
